# Business Questions

This project analyzes the Maven Pizza Place sales dataset using SQL Server to answer business questions related to sales performance, revenue, products, order behavior, and business opportunities.

---

## 1. Data Exploration

### Q1. Orders and Peak Hours

* How many orders do we have each day?
* Are there any peak ordering hours?

### Q2. Pizzas per Order and Bestsellers

* How many pizzas are typically included in an order?
* Which pizzas are the bestsellers?

### Q3. Revenue and Sales Seasonality

* How much revenue was generated during the year?
* Can we identify any seasonal patterns in sales?

### Q4. Menu and Promotion Opportunities

* Are there pizzas with low sales that should be reviewed?
* Which pizza categories could provide promotion opportunities?
